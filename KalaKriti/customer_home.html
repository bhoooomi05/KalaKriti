Our Mission

KalaKriti bridges the gap between traditional artisans and the digital world. We empower rural and tribal artists by giving them direct access to buyers, skill-based workshops, and digital literacy — ensuring that India's rich cultural legacy continues to thrive sustainably.

Market Insights

The global handicrafts market is valued at USD 740 billion (2024) and is expected to reach USD 983 billion by 2030. In India alone, over 7 million artisans are part of this ecosystem, yet most lack digital visibility. KalaKriti bridges this gap.

Sources: Grand View Research (2024), Fortune Business Insights (2025)
